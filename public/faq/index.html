Mission

We believe that innovations in biology should be accessible, affordable, and open to everyone. We’re building a community biology lab for amateurs, inventors, entrepreneurs, and anyone who wants to experiment and learn.

BioCurious is a Biosafety Level 1 (BSL-1) lab — equivalent to a high school biology classroom — open to people from all backgrounds.

All our meetings, events, and projects are organized with Meetup. Sign up at meetup.com/biocurious to stay informed.
