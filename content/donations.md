We'll make good use of consumables, gear, and reagents. Consider also donating to our friends at [BioLink Depot](https://www.biolinkdepot.org/).

Contact us at [info@biocurious.org](mailto:info@biocurious.org) before sending anything.

## ✓ We want
- FBS, HPLC columns, lab consumables
- Working equipment and useful reagents

## ✗ We don't want
- Fluorescent bulbs, cadmium chloride, HPLC waste, hazardous materials
