## Our Mission

We believe that innovations in biology should be accessible, affordable, and
open to everyone. We're building a community biology lab for amateurs,
inventors, entrepreneurs, and anyone who wants to experiment and learn.

## What We Are

1. A laboratory and co-working space for the community to be able to
   cheaply access specialized equipment, materials, and space
   to experiment and advance biotech-related projects
2. A training center for lab skills, with an emphasis on safety
3. A meeting place for citizen scientists, hobbyists, activists, and students
