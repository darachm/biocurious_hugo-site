## Our Mission

We believe that innovations in biology should be accessible, affordable, and open to everyone. We're building a community biology lab for amateurs, inventors, entrepreneurs, and anyone who wants to experiment and learn.

## What We Are

1. A complete working laboratory and technical library for entrepreneurs to cheaply access equipment, materials, and co-working space
2. A training center for biotechniques, with an emphasis on safety
3. A meeting place for citizen scientists, hobbyists, activists, and students
