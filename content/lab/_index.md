# Wet lab

## An aside about biosafety 

We run this facility with BSL-1 level of biosafety controls.
Every member is admitted with a specific project that undergoes a process
of review.
A Safety Committee reviews the project, raises concerns about any hazards,
and works with the applicant to find mitigations to enable the work to be done
in a way that is safe for the lab users, the community, and the environment.

To summarize how it may apply to your desired project, 
no work is permitted that involves an agent that can cause disease in a 
typical human.
This means we do not allow any work with viral transfection, nor mammalian cell
lines that have actively mobile elements, nor any samples from humans that have
not been sufficiently inactivated to eliminate any disease risk 
(aka universal precautions).

Also forbidden is any use of bacteriophages, bacteriophage-related 
molecular-biology or elements there of, or fruit flies.

This is the main lab.
Here you'll find most of the equipment, reagents, and space for you to use.
If you're a member of the lab you can use any of the bench space when you want
to (within reason), so make sure you only use what you need and cleanup after 
you are done. It's shared, but usually there's about twice as much bench space
as people.

Some members pay extra to specifically reserve bench space in this main lab
area, so those areas are off limits. Everything else is up for reasonable use.
Pay attention to signage indicated a "Reserved Bench". As of May 2026 there's
only two reserved benches.

Here is a partial list of equipment you can probably expect 
(but must _verify_ is still available) there:

- reasonable PPE (gloves, glasses/goggles, labcoats if you desire)
- pipettes and free pipette tips
- thermocyclers for PCR

Specialized equipment that you need to talk to us about using to get 
trained on our use and practices in this lab:

- Waters HPLC
- Accuri flow cytometer
- Microscope
