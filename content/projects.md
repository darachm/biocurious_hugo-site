We have had many projects in the past and are always organizing more.

## 🧬 CRISPR Class
**Newbie friendly** — Crash course on molecular biology and cloning. From zero to disrupting a gene in a month.

## 🧀 Real Vegan Cheese
**Recruiting** — What is the essence of cheese, and can we make it in a flask? An ongoing community bioengineering project.

## 🔧 Fix or Teardown?
**Self-directed** — We often have broken gear to fix or teardown into useful components. Like hardware? We need help.
