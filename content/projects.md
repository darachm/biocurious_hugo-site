We have had many projects in the past. We're in a bit of a lull right now,
but we hope to adjust our rules to enable motivated membership to lead new
projects.

The Real Vegan Cheese project (below) is still recruiting and meeting online,
so see our meetup page for more specifics.

Interested in starting your own project? [Become a member](/membership/) and 
submit your project for safety committee review.

# Past Projects

## 🧀 Real Vegan Cheese
**Recruiting** — What is the essence of cheese, and can we make it in a flask?
An ongoing community bioengineering project.
See the Meetup page for upcoming meetings.

## 🧬 CRISPR Class
This was a **newbie friendly** crash course on molecular biology and 
cloning, focused around the goal of disrupting a gene in a human cell-line 
_in vitro_ culture

## 🔧 Fix or Teardown?
**Self-directed** — We often have broken gear to fix or teardown into 
useful components. Like hardware? We need help.
